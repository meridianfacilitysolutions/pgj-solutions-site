How we work

A facility manager shouldn’t have to coordinate a different vendor for every task. We take responsibility for the full scope, with our own management and on-site quality control.

  1. 1

    Scope

    We review the statement of work, walk the site where possible, and confirm tasks, frequencies, hours, and access rules in writing.

  2. 2

    Staff and schedule

    PG&J’s own employees do the core cleaning work, supervised on site by PG&J. We set the schedule, supplies, and site contacts. Specialty work, such as equipment repair, goes to licensed partners, and PG&J stays responsible for it.

  3. 3

    Quality control

    We inspect the work against the written scope, track issues until they are closed, and report back, so you have one contact for every question.
