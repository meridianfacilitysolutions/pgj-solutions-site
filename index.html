How we work

A facility manager shouldn’t have to coordinate a different vendor for every task. We take responsibility for the full scope, with our own management and on-site quality control.

  1. 1

    Scope

    We review the statement of work, walk the site where possible, and confirm tasks, frequencies, hours, and access rules in writing.

  2. 2

    Coordinate

    We assign the crew to do the work, then set the schedule, supplies, and site contacts.

  3. 3

    Quality control

    We inspect the work against the written scope, track issues until they are closed, and report back, so you have one contact for every question.

About PG&J Solutions

Simeon Meehan started PG&J Solutions because too many jobs get passed between vendors who don’t coordinate with each other. The company is set up so a facility manager has one person to call, who follows through and takes responsibility for the outcome.

Accountability. We own the result, whoever performs the task. Respect. Courteous, professional conduct with every customer. Reliability. Work done on the agreed schedule, to the written scope. Transparency. Clear communication and no surprises.
