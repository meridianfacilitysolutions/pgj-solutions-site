How we work

Facility managers should not have to coordinate a different vendor for every task. PG&J Solutions takes responsibility for the full scope, and services are delivered through vetted subcontractors under our management and quality control.

  1. 1

    Scope

    We review the statement of work, walk the site where possible, and confirm tasks, frequencies, hours, and access rules in writing.

  2. 2

    Coordinate

    We coordinate vetted subcontractors who perform the work under our management and quality control, and set the schedule, supplies, and site contacts.

  3. 3

    Quality control

    We inspect against the scope, track issues to closure, and report back, so you have one contact for every question.

About PG&J Solutions

Simeon Meehan founded PG&J Solutions after seeing how often businesses get passed between vendors who don’t coordinate with each other. PG&J Solutions is built so a facility manager has a single point of contact who follows through on the work and takes responsibility for the outcome.

Accountability. We own the result, whoever performs the task. Respect. Courteous, professional conduct with every customer. Reliability. Work done on the agreed schedule, to the written scope. Transparency. Clear communication and no surprises.
