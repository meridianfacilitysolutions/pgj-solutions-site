PG&J SOLUTIONS

Capability Statement · Janitorial, Floor Care & Grounds Maintenance · Fairfield, California

Company overview

PG&J Solutions is a small business based in Fairfield, California, launching in January 2027. We provide janitorial and custodial services, commercial carpet and floor care, and routine grounds maintenance for public buildings and commercial properties in Solano County, near Travis AFB, and across Northern California. Our approach is one point of accountability: we plan the work, PG&J employees perform the core cleaning under PG&J on-site supervision, and the owner inspects the results in person.

Core competencies

  • Janitorial & custodial (561720): recurring cleaning of offices, public buildings, restrooms, break rooms, and common areas; restocking, trash removal, and high-touch disinfecting.
  • Commercial carpet & floor care (561740): vacuuming, spot treatment, carpet extraction, and hard-floor scrubbing and refinishing.
  • Grounds maintenance (561730): mowing, edging, and litter and debris removal. Routine upkeep only; no landscape installation.
  • Equipment repair & maintenance (811310)AFTER LAUNCH Coordinated through licensed specialty partners, with PG&J as the single point of contact.

Differentiators

  • Single point of accountability for scheduling, issues, and reporting.
  • Owner-led on-site quality control: the owner brings over 10 years of hands-on cleaning experience and inspects job sites in person.
  • Own workforce: PG&J employees perform the core cleaning work, supervised on site by PG&J.
  • Written scope and inspection reporting: work is checked against what was agreed, and issues are tracked until closed.
  • Local to Travis AFB and Solano County for fast response and site visits.
  • Evening and weekend scheduling to fit facility operating hours.
